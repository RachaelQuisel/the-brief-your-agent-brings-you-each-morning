### The three ideas (tape this to your monitor)

1. **Checking is the agent's job. Deciding is yours.** The agent watches and ranks. You decide.
2. **Drafting is free. Sending is not.** Prepare at full speed. A named person approves what leaves.
3. **The brief must name what it did not touch.** Judgment you can see is judgment you can correct.

### The numbers that make the case

From this week's stories: Anthropic opened the Claude Duet waitlist on October 21 for United States Pro and Max subscribers, aimed at professionals and small business owners, with approval required before the agent sends, pays, signs, or deletes. The same week, Sierra and Stripe showed a voice agent taking a phone payment where the card digits travel straight to Stripe and the agent receives only the payment status and the last four digits. One pattern in both stories: the agent works fast everywhere except at the consequential step, where a person, a fixed script, or a written limit takes over.

The highlighted rule and the completed never list are the proof the design is real.
